An evaluation of [eesm.com.au](https://eesm.com.au/) reveals a fundamental disconnect: **Energize Electrical Safety Management (EESM) has an impressive commercial track record** (including projects for ADCO, Hutchinson Builders, Mainbrace, Coles, Woolworths, Bond University, and Suncorp Stadium), **yet the website functions as an outdated, passive 2019 brochure.**

Currently, **there is no Capability Statement on the site**, and the quote request pathway is an unoptimized, generic contact form. To turn this site into an engine for commercial tenders and maintenance contracts, it requires structural usability fixes, modernized trust signals, and dedicated lead-capture funnels.

## 2. Content & Trust Deficits

### The Value Proposition is Vague

* **Current Copy:** *"50 YEARS EXPERIENCE. We pride ourselves on our reputation and reliability, consistently providing a high level of service through efficiency and ingenuity."*
* **The Problem:** This relies on generic corporate platitudes. The claim "50 Years Experience" contradicts the company's founding date (2003) without clarifying that it refers to *combined leadership experience*.
* **The Fix:** Lead with operational scale and specific B2B positioning:
> *"Commercial Electrical Contracting & Compliance Management Across South East Queensland. Director-supervised delivery for Tier-1 builders, commercial assets, and industrial facilities."*



### Mixed Market Signals

The services menu lists **"Domestic Services"** alongside complex statutory work like **"Thermal Imaging"** and **"Electrical Safety Audits"**.

* High-value commercial developers (e.g., ADCO, Broad) do not want a domestic electrician.
* Commercial facility managers need compliance packages, not residential wiring.
* **Fix:** Separate the offerings cleanly into **Commercial Contracting & Projects** vs. **Statutory Safety & Compliance Maintenance**. Remove or demote "Domestic" to a minor sub-tier.

### Underutilized Social Proof

EESM has exceptional testimonials from Michael Bourke (*Wood & Grieve Engineers*), Dilan Perera (*Brisbane Racing Club*), and Tom O’Sullivan (*Coles Group*). However:

* They are presented as plain blocks of text at the bottom of the homepage.
* There are no client logos, project photography, or project metrics.
* **Fix:** Add corporate logos above the fold and pair each quote with the actual facility completed (e.g., Eagle Farm Racecourse electrical upgrade).

## 3. Optimizing the "Request a Quote" Funnel

The current form asks: *"Do you need help with electrical work? Fill out this form to get in touch."* This reads like a residential contractor form, discouraging estimators who need to submit complex tender packages.

```
Current Experience (High Abandonment):
[ Homepage / Button ] ---> [ Generic 4-Field Form: Name, Email, Phone, Message ]
                                      ↓
                     No document uploads, no scope definition

```

### Proposed Multi-Track Quote Funnel

```
Proposed Dual-Track Architecture:

[ Primary CTA: "Request a Project / Compliance Quote" ]
                           │
       ┌───────────────────┴───────────────────┐
       ▼                                       ▼
 Track A: Commercial Tenders & Projects   Track B: Facility Safety & Audits
 • Tender scope / package title           • Facility type (Industrial, Retail, Strata)
 • Drawing / Plan upload (PDF/DWG/ZIP)    • Testing type (RCD, Thermal, Test & Tag)
 • Required completion date               • Frequency (One-off vs Annual Program)
 • Tender submission deadline             • Site address / access requirements

```

### Critical Form UX Improvements:

1. **File Upload Support:** Add a multi-file upload button (`PDF`, `DWG`, `ZIP` up to 50MB) labeled **"Upload Tender Documents / Electrical Single Line Diagrams"**.
2. **Director-Led Trust Badges Beside Form:**
* *"Every quote is personally reviewed by Managing Directors Dan Monti & Jason Edwards."*
* Display license details: **Electrical Contractor Lic # 70739**, Master Electricians / CM3 / Safety Management accreditation badges.


3. **Response SLA:** State a clear turnaround time (e.g., *"Tender responses acknowledged within 4 business hours"*).

## 4. Designing the "Capability Statement" Lead Magnet

A Capability Statement is the standard currency in Australian commercial construction. Estimators and contract administrators download them to vet subcontractors for preferred tenderer lists. **EESM currently lacks this asset entirely.**

### Strategic Placement

* **Sticky Navigation Bar:** Add a secondary button in the top navigation: `Download Capability Statement [PDF]`.
* **Hero Section:** Complement the primary CTA:
* Primary: `[ Request a Tender Quote ]` (High-intent)
* Secondary: `[ Download 2026 Capability Statement ]` (Mid/Top-of-funnel)


* **On the "Work" Page:** Place a mid-page banner: *"Need full project sheets, plant equipment lists, and insurance certificates? Download our complete Capability Statement."*

### Lead Capture Mechanics (Soft Gate)

Do not create a lengthy barrier. Keep friction minimal:

* **Fields:** First Name, Work Email, Company Name, and Role (*Estimator / Project Manager / Facility Manager / Other*).
* **Delivery:** Provide an **instant browser download** upon submit, while automatically dispatching a copy to their email with direct contact details for Dan Monti and Jason Edwards.

### What the PDF Capability Statement Must Include:

1. **Core Competencies:** Commercial construction, HV upgrades, main switchboards, statutory compliance testing.
2. **Director Profiles & Track Record:** 50+ combined years of direct site management.
3. **Key Project Matrix:** Project name, client (ADCO, Hutchinson, Coles), value band, scope of work.
4. **Compliance & Safety Systems:** AS/NZS 4801 / ISO 45001 alignment, WHS management plans.
5. **Insurances & Licences:** Electrical Contractor Licence # 70739, $20M Public Liability confirmation, WorkCover Queensland compliance.

Replace the current flat, duplicate navigation with a role-oriented layout:

```
[ Header Navigation ]
├── Services ▾
│   ├── Commercial Contracting & Fit-Outs (HV Upgrades, Switchboards, Retail)
│   └── Electrical Safety & Compliance (Thermal Imaging, RCD, Test & Tag, Emergency Lights)

├── About Us (Director-Led Model, Safety & Systems)
├── Capability Statement [Direct Download / Lead Capture]
└── [ REQUEST A QUOTE ] (High-contrast Button)

```

### Revamping the "Work" Page

The current page is an unformatted text list of 40+ projects ending in 2018. Transform it into a scannable credential showcase:

* **Filter Tabs:** `All`, `Tier-1 Commercial`, `Shopping Centres & Retail`, `Institutional & Health`.
* **Case Study Cards:** Include high-res installation photography, client logo, site location, and key deliverables (e.g., *"Main switchboard replacement with zero unscheduled downtime"*).
* **Recent Updates:** Populate the page with projects from 2020–2026 to prove current market activity.
