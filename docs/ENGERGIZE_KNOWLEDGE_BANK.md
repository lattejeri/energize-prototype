## 4. Comprehensive Service Offerings & Technical Compliance

Energize Industries provides dedicated, specialist testing, compliance, auditing, and maintenance services. Every service adheres strictly to Australian/New Zealand Standards and statutory Workplace Health & Safety regulations.

---

### 4.1 Portable Appliance Testing & Tagging (Test & Tag)
- **Primary Compliance Standards:**
  - **AS/NZS 3760:** *In-service safety inspection and testing of electrical equipment*
  - **AS/NZS 3012:** *Electrical installations – Construction and demolition sites*
  - *Electrical Safety Regulation 2002 (Queensland)* & *Work Health and Safety Act / Regulation*
- **Scope & Scope of Testing:**
  - Inspection and testing of all single-phase and polyphase (240V – 415V) portable electrical equipment, flexible extension cords, portable power outlets (EPODs), and industrial power tools.
  - Covers equipment in-service, returning to service after repair, or available for hire/resale.
- **Technical Procedures:**
  - Physical visual inspection for mechanical damage, cord fraying, plug integrity, and strain relief.
  - Electrical parameter testing: earth continuity, insulation resistance, earth leakage current, and polarity checks.
  - Attachment of durable, non-reusable compliance tags indicating test date, re-test due date, technician license, and asset ID.
  - Electronic capture and provision of an itemized asset register and compliance logbook.
- **Testing Frequency Classifications:**
  - *Class 1 Work (Construction and Demolition Sites):* Tested every 3 months in accordance with AS/NZS 3012.
  - *Class 2, 3, and 4 Work (Factories, Workshops, Commercial Offices, Service Environments):* Tested at 6-month, 12-month, 2-year, or 5-year intervals as dictated by risk assessments under the Queensland Electrical Safety Regulation.
- **Exemptions & Non-Testable Items:**
  - Fixed / hard-wired equipment, items installed at heights exceeding 2.5 metres, new demo stock in warehouses, or equipment requiring destructive dismantling.
- **Personnel Qualifications:** Testing is executed exclusively by qualified electrical mechanics and licensed technicians specially trained in AS/NZS 3760 interpretation.

---

### 4.2 Infrared Thermal Imaging (Thermography)
- **Principle & Purpose:**
  Infrared thermography converts invisible thermal energy (heat signatures) emitted by electrical switchboards, busbars, circuit breakers, and mechanical components into visual thermographic images. Thermal anomalies indicate high-resistance connections, phase imbalances, overloaded circuits, or component fatigue prior to physical failure.
- **Operational Execution:**
  - Executed on live, fully energized switchboards and plant without requiring operational shutdowns or interruption to client operations.
  - High-resolution radiometric infrared cameras paired with calibrated optical reference photos.
  - Inspection covers: Main switchboards (MSBs), distribution boards (DBs), motor control centres (MCCs), control panels, busway joints, contactors, and mechanical drive bearings.
- **Insurance Risk Management & Financial Benefits:**
  - Underwriters and commercial property insurers frequently mandate periodic thermal imaging audits as a prerequisite for property and business interruption insurance.
  - Mitigates multi-million-dollar fire risks, prevents catastrophic equipment outages, and provides documentation supporting reduced commercial insurance premiums.
- **Comprehensive Reporting Standards:**
  - High-quality, full-colour thermal analysis report provided in bound hard copy and searchable digital PDF format.
  - Side-by-side comparison of thermal image and optical photograph.
  - Detailed temperature delta ($\Delta T$) analysis above ambient and phase baseline.
  - Categorized, prioritized hierarchy of recommended remedial actions (Immediate, Urgent, Monitor).
  - Recommended inspection cycle: Minimum annual inspection; biannual (every 6 months) for high-load or mission-critical industrial environments.

### 4.4 Residual Current Device (RCD) / Safety Switch Testing
- **Compliance Standards & Legislation:**
  - **AS/NZS 3760:** Section on testing of Residual Current Devices.
  - *Workplace Health and Safety Regulations* & *Electrical Safety Act 2002*.
- **Role & Importance:**
  - RCDs are life-safety devices designed to detect infinitesimal current leakages to earth (e.g., passing through a human body) and disconnect electricity supply in milliseconds, preventing lethal electrocution.
- **Two-Stage Testing Protocol:**
  1. **Manual Push-Button Test (Operational Verification):** Verifies the internal mechanical tripping mechanism operates freely.
  2. **Millisecond Trip-Time & Current Leakage Test (Precision Measurement):** Using specialized, calibrated RCD test instruments to inject a controlled fault current (10mA, 30mA, etc.) and record the precise disconnection time in milliseconds (ms) to ensure compliance with maximum tripping thresholds.
- **Scope:** Switchboard-mounted fixed RCDs and portable inline RCD safety switches across all distribution panels.
- **Documentation:** Full test data recorded and incorporated into statutory safety registers.

### 4.5 Electrical Safety Audits & Compliance Inspections
- **Facility Safety Audits:** Detailed diagnostic evaluation of existing electrical infrastructure for commercial buildings, retail complexes, and residences to identify latent electrical hazards, code violations, and shock risks.
- **Ceramic Wire Fuse Upgrades:**
  - In properties over 15–20 years old, existing switchboards frequently still feature obsolete ceramic rewireable fuses.
  - If inappropriate fuse wire is fitted during maintenance, the circuit will fail to clear under fault conditions, directly initiating catastrophic switchboard fires.
  - Energize provides switchboard replacements and retrofits, upgrading legacy boards to modern modular enclosures equipped with combined Residual Current Circuit Breakers with Overcurrent Protection (RCBOs).
- **Legislative Alignment:** Audits align facility practices with OH&S obligations, WorkSafe regulations, and insurer criteria.
