Stakeholder alignment

Why infrared scanning is critical for your role

For commercial facility & property managers

Eliminate liability and preserve asset continuity

A switchboard failure stops operations and exposes asset owners to severe tenant compensation claims. Periodic thermal audits provide verifiable audit trails that prove due diligence to executive boards and insurers.

• Complete itemized asset registers provided after every inspection
For head contractors & project engineers

Defect-free baseline commissioning

Conducting thermal scans during practical completion verifies that newly installed main switchboards, sub-mains, and breaker terminations operate under balanced phase load with zero latent manufacturing defects.

• Baseline temperature records embedded into handover documentation
Inspection protocol

Our three-stage thermographic process

01

Live radiometric scan [PLEASE CHECK]

High-resolution radiometric infrared cameras paired with calibrated optical reference photos scan all switchboards, busbars, and contactors under normal operational load.

02

Delta-T anomaly evaluation [PLEASE CHECK]

Measuring heat rise ($\Delta T$) above ambient and phase baseline to isolate loose terminations, internal component fatigue, or unbalanced electrical phases.

03

Prioritized remedial dossier [PLEASE CHECK]

Provision of a searchable digital report with side-by-side thermal/optical photos, exact temperature metrics, and categorized rectifications (Immediate, Urgent, Monitor).
