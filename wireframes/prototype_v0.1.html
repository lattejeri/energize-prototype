Infrared Thermal Imaging & Switchboard Fault Detection

Detect latent hot spots, high-resistance joints, and overloaded phases on live switchboards before catastrophic failure or electrical fires occur. Fulfill mandatory commercial property insurance underwriting requirements.

Why Thermal Imaging is Essential for Asset Managers

Zero Operational Downtime: Conducted completely live during peak operating hours under normal load—meaning zero interruption to your tenants or plant operations.
Mandatory Insurer Compliance: Underwriters increasingly mandate annual thermographic audits as a condition of commercial building and business interruption coverage.

Our Thermographic Inspection Process

01
Live Radiometric Scan
High-resolution infrared scanning of MSBs, distribution boards, busbars, and contactors under load.
02
Delta-T Anomaly Evaluation
Measuring heat rises above ambient and phase baseline to diagnose loose terminations or phase imbalance.
